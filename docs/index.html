THE METHOD

From risk estimates to decisions.

A transparent workflow designed to expose assumptions and trade-offs.

01 ESTIMATE
⌁

Multiple risk forecasts

Compare long-horizon covariance, short-horizon covariance and EWMA. Different market windows tell different risk stories.

02 CALIBRATE
⟡

Historical prediction errors

Use fully completed months to measure where reference portfolios' realized volatility exceeded model forecasts.

03 OPTIMIZE
⤧

Scenario-robust allocation

Keep the portfolio inside each modeled covariance limit, subject to positions, turnover and risk-budget constraints.

04 EXPLAIN
≋

Expose the opportunity cost

Show expected return sacrificed for risk protection, risk contribution shifts and the estimated cost of rebalancing.

i
Research boundary

In historical tests, stricter robust constraints often reduced realized risk-limit violations, but did not consistently improve realized utility versus simply lowering a conventional risk budget. Historical calibration is descriptive — not a formal confidence guarantee.

portfolio_example.py
from portfolio_risk_lab import PortfolioAnalyzer

# returns: daily decimal returns DataFrame
lab = PortfolioAnalyzer(
    returns,
    cash_asset="Cash_Proxy",
    risk_budget=0.12,
    max_weight=0.40
)

result = lab.compare(
    methods=["standard", "scenario_robust"],
    uncertainty="historical_calibration"
)

print(result.risk_budget_analysis)
